# olympic-performance-analysis

## Data Visualization
Map note: The world map plots 207 of 218 NOCs. “Chinese Taipei” was manually matched to Taiwan in Tableau. Ten entities could not be placed because they are not current sovereign countries: Unified Team (1992), Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia, Serbia and Montenegro, Netherlands Antilles, Korea (Unified Team), Individual Neutral Athletes, Individual Olympic Athletes, Refugee Olympic Team (two NOC codes), and the Russian Olympic Committee (kept separate from Russia, consistent with the rest of the analysis). Together they account for 286 of the 11,106 medals (about 2.6%), so the map slightly understates total medals, most notably for Russia. These medals are still included in all non-map charts and KPIs.
